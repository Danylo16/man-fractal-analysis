 # Abstract

The 2024 student research investigated daily electricity demand in Rivne region using 1,553 observations, fractal and spectral methods, temperature correlation, and statistical and machine-learning forecasting. The original study reported annual and weekly seasonality and explored SARIMAX and XGBoost across multiple forecast horizons. The 2026 revision seeks to reproduce and critically evaluate those findings; original data access and independent replication remain outstanding.
