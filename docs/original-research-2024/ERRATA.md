# Errata and methodological notes — 2024 study

The original manuscript and its English translation preserve the historical research. The following notes distinguish corrections from the original reported findings.

- For H = 0.7259, the arithmetic value of 2 − H is 1.2741, not 1.2471. The relationship itself requires appropriate self-affinity assumptions.
- Table 2.1 reports Dickey–Fuller p-values of 2.2839859344358332 × 10⁻²⁰ and 2.3506358267087765 × 10⁻²⁸. The exponents are present in the original DOCX mathematical notation. An earlier revision of these notes misread the notation; that correction has been withdrawn. The original statistical outputs remain unavailable. The reported p-values do not by themselves establish that all test assumptions were satisfied.
- R/S slopes alone cannot establish long-range dependence: trends, seasonality and short-range dependence may affect estimates.
- A single shuffled series is insufficient for robust inference; repeated permutations and dependence-preserving controls are preferable.
- The estimated annual spectral period of 388.25 days may reflect finite frequency resolution.
- Forecast evaluation needs chronological validation, simple baselines and explicit availability assumptions for future temperature covariates.
- The original electricity-demand dataset is unavailable. The 2026 S&P 500 experiment is a separate exploratory extension, not a replication of the historical results.
